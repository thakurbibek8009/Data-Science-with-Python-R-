# Analysis Preregistration

## Research question
Write one falsifiable question before examining the outcome relationships.

## Hypotheses
Define the null and alternative hypotheses and the expected direction, if justified.

## Population, sample, and exclusions
State the target population, unit of analysis, inclusion rules, exclusion rules, and stopping rule.

## Variables and measures
Define outcome, predictors, controls, transformations, and missing-data handling.

## Analysis plan
Name the statistical tests or models, assumptions, alpha level, effect sizes, uncertainty intervals, and robustness checks.

## Deviations
Record every later deviation from this plan with a timestamp and reason.
